Set it up around your league and use it as the live draft assistant beside the room. What it computes is a Draft-State Valuation: what each remaining player is worth to *your* roster at *this* pick.

While the league focuses on the next pick, GREEN18 helps you focus on what the previous picks changed.

How GREEN18 Works

  1. Set Your League.

    Configure the scoring, roster structure and league format that materially affect player value.

  2. Shape Your Draft.

    Set the preferences that matter to you, instead of drafting from one universal list.

  3. Start Drafting.

    As players are selected, remove them from the board. GREEN18 recalculates each remaining player’s margin over replacement.

  4. Make the Pick.

    One live view of the players that still matter. You still make the call.

Questions

Does GREEN18 import my Sleeper draft?

No. GREEN18 does not integrate with any league platform. You enter your league settings in GREEN18 and use it alongside your draft.

Can I run GREEN18 at the same time as my draft room?

Yes. GREEN18 is built to be used beside your existing draft, so you can track the remaining player pool as picks are made.

Is GREEN18 available on Android?

GREEN18 is currently focused on iPhone.
