Questions

How can someone with no fantasy football preparation compete in an office league draft?

Most of the advantage in a draft comes from reading the current situation rather than from memorizing players. GREEN18 applies the league’s scoring and roster rules to the available player pool and re-ranks it as picks are recorded, so a manager who has done no offseason research can still see which available player fits their roster at the moment they are on the clock.

How long does it take to set GREEN18 up before an office draft?

Setup is entering the league’s scoring and roster settings and any personal preferences. It is a short configuration step done before the draft, not a research project, and the settings can be reused for the same league.

Does GREEN18 connect to the platform where the office league is hosted?

No. GREEN18 does not connect to, sync with, or import from any fantasy platform. It is an independent companion used alongside whatever platform the league runs on, and picks are recorded in GREEN18 as they happen.

Does GREEN18 draft for you?

No. GREEN18 does not auto-draft or make selections. It reorganizes the remaining player board and shows which available players fit the roster; the manager makes every pick themselves.
