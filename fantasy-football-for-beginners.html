How GREEN18 Works

  1. Set Your League.

    Tell GREEN18 how your league actually works — scoring, roster structure, format.

  2. Shape Your Draft.

    Set the preferences that matter to you. No fantasy vocabulary test required.

  3. Start Drafting.

    As players are selected, remove them from the board. GREEN18 recalculates the remaining opportunity.

  4. Make the Pick.

    One live view of the players that still matter, instead of six browser tabs.

Questions

Can I use GREEN18 if I’ve never played fantasy football?

Yes. The product is designed to make the available player pool easier to navigate during the draft.

Does GREEN18 automatically make my picks?

No. You remain the manager. GREEN18 helps organize the decision.

Do I need to know fantasy football terminology?

Some basic understanding helps, but GREEN18 is designed to reduce the amount of information you need to manually process during the draft.
