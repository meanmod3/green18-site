The One Idea Worth Learning First.

Here it is, and it is the whole game: a player is only worth what he gives you over the player you could take instead.

That second player is the replacement — the next-best option at the same position when your turn comes back around. The gap between them is your margin over replacement, and it is the only number that actually wins you a week.

This is why the loudest name is not always the right pick. Six equally good running backs on the board means taking one costs you almost nothing in the way of missed opportunity. Two good ones left and a steep drop after them means waiting is expensive. That drop-off is positional scarcity, and it moves every single pick.

You do not have to compute any of this. You just have to know that it is what the draft is doing while you look at it. Anything you want spelled out is in the glossary.

Not “is he good?” — “how much better is he than what I’d get instead?”

Playing With People Who’ve Done This for Years?

That is most first drafts, and it is more survivable than it looks.

Questions

Can I use GREEN18 if I’ve never played fantasy football?

Yes. The product is designed to make the available player pool easier to navigate during the draft, and it applies the league rules and the current draft state on the manager’s behalf rather than requiring the manager to reason about them.

Does GREEN18 automatically make my picks?

No. You remain the manager. GREEN18 helps organize the decision.

Do I need to know fantasy football terminology?

Some basic understanding helps, but GREEN18 is designed to reduce the amount of information you need to manually process during the draft.

What is the single most useful idea for a first-time drafter?

A player is worth what he produces above the player you would otherwise take at the same position, which is called margin over replacement. A position with several similar players remaining is cheap to wait on, while a position with a steep quality drop after the next few players is expensive to wait on.
