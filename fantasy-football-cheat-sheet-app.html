The Old Cheat Sheet vs. the GREEN18 Cheat Sheet

Same purpose. Four steps against eight — and the last one is the difference.

The Old Cheat Sheet

1. Rank players. 2. Start the draft. 3. Cross names off. 4. Keep following approximately the same order.

The GREEN18 Cheat Sheet

1. Understand the league. 2. Understand your preferences. 3. Establish the available player market. 4. Start the draft. 5. Observe every selection. 6. Recalculate the remaining opportunity. 7. Re-rank the board. 8. Repeat after every pick.

Why the Extra Steps Matter

Crossing a player off the board isn’t enough. The players below him just changed too. The sheet becomes part of the draft instead of merely being brought into it.

Questions

What is the difference between a fantasy football cheat sheet and GREEN18?

A traditional cheat sheet is a snapshot: players are ranked before the draft, and the list stays in that order while names are crossed off. GREEN18 keeps recalculating instead. It applies the league settings, records each selection, recalculates the remaining opportunity, and re-ranks the available board after every pick.

Why does removing one player change the rest of the cheat sheet?

Because value on a draft board is relative. Removing a player reduces the depth remaining at his position, changes which alternatives are left, and changes the odds that a comparable player is still available at the next pick. The names that remain are worth something different than they were a pick earlier.

Is GREEN18 a fantasy football cheat sheet?

GREEN18 can serve the same purpose, but it is designed to be more responsive than a traditional static cheat sheet.

Do the rankings change?

The product is built around a changing player board that responds to the draft as available players and team context change.

Can beginners use it?

Yes. GREEN18 can simplify the draft by concentrating the available player pool into one decision-focused view.
