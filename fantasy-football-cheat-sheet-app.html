Questions

Is GREEN18 a fantasy football cheat sheet?

GREEN18 can serve the same purpose, but it is designed to be more responsive than a traditional static cheat sheet.

Do the rankings change?

The product is built around a changing player board that responds to the draft as available players and team context change.

Can beginners use it?

Yes. GREEN18 can simplify the draft by concentrating the available player pool into one decision-focused view.
