PPR Is a Setting. Not a Strategy.

Knowing that receptions are valuable is only the beginning. How PPR scoring re-prices a player pool is settled before anyone picks.

The real questions happen during the draft.

Which receiver?

Which running back?

How much depth is left above the next tier?

Should you take the value now?

Can you afford to wait until your next pick?

GREEN18 is built around those decisions — which is what a live draft assistant is for.

Static Rankings Stop at the Starting Line.

Most fantasy football rankings answer one question:

Who looked valuable before the draft started?

Your real draft creates different questions.

Which positions are thinning fastest — and how does that compare to every other position?

Who is likely to survive to your next pick?

Did the last five picks collapse a tier and change what the board is worth?

That is Draft-State Valuation: pricing a player by the state of the draft in front of you rather than by a rank set weeks ago.

Why a PPR Board Has to Move.

Live, Not Static

The board responds to the draft instead of presenting the same rankings from Pick 1 through Pick 150.

Built Around Your League

Reception scoring changes where replacement level sits at every position, so a player can be worth very different amounts in two leagues drafting the same night. GREEN18 starts with the league you’re actually playing.

Margin Over Replacement

The number that matters is not a player’s projection but how far it sits above the player you could take instead. That margin is what shrinks while you wait.

How GREEN18 Works

  1. Set Your League.

    Configure the scoring — full PPR, half, or your own — plus roster structure and format.

  2. Shape Your Draft.

    Set the preferences that matter to you, instead of drafting from one universal list.

  3. Start Drafting.

    As players are selected, remove them. Players rise. Players fall. Tiers collapse. Roster needs change.

  4. Make the Pick.

    One live view of the players that still matter, instead of six browser tabs. The same board always produces the same answer.

Your Scoring Belongs in the Board.

A universal ranking cannot represent every league, because it has to assume one replacement level and your league has its own.

GREEN18 starts from the premise that your scoring system should materially influence the players you are considering — the PPR value adjustment calculator shows how far the same pool moves between formats.

Once the draft begins, the opportunity cost of every pick is measured against what your own board says is still available. It is the same principle behind treating ADP as a starting line rather than the answer.

When You Already Have the Receivers.

PPR pushes rooms toward receivers, and that creates the most common mid-draft question in the format: the best player available is a fourth receiver and your roster no longer needs one.

Scarcity is measured against your roster, not the league’s. A cliff you are already standing above costs you nothing. When you already have two wide receivers walks that decision through.

Draft the League You Joined.

Reception scoring is your league’s rule. Your board should know it.
