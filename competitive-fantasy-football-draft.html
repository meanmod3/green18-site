The Edge Is Context.

Player evaluation matters.

But once the draft begins, every pick changes the opportunity set.

GREEN18 helps you evaluate the remaining board through the state of the draft instead of relying exclusively on the consensus order everyone else can see.

That is the whole idea behind the fantasy football draft assistant.

Stop Drafting the Same Rankings as the Room.

Consensus is useful.

Consensus is also available to everyone.

GREEN18 is designed to layer your league, roster, preferences, availability, and draft movement onto that baseline.

Which is why ADP is the starting line, not the answer.

How GREEN18 Works

  1. Set Your League.

    Configure the scoring, roster structure and format that materially affect player value.

  2. Shape Your Draft.

    Set the preferences that matter to you instead of drafting from a universal list.

  3. Start Drafting.

    Remove players as they are selected. GREEN18 recalculates the remaining opportunity.

  4. Make the Pick.

    One live view of the players that still matter, instead of rankings, notes and tabs.
