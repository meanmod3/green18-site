Questions

Can I set up GREEN18 the day of my draft?

Yes. Configure your league settings and preferences in GREEN18, then open it beside your draft and start removing players as they are selected.

Do I need to study rankings before I use GREEN18?

Some basic understanding helps, but GREEN18 is designed to reduce the amount of information you need to manually process during the draft.

Can I use GREEN18 with the league I already play in?

GREEN18 is designed to work alongside the league where you already play. Configure the relevant league settings in GREEN18 and use it during your draft.
