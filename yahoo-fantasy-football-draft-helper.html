Questions

Does GREEN18 sync with my Yahoo draft?

No. GREEN18 has no platform integration. You configure your league inside GREEN18 and use it as an independent board beside your draft.

Can I keep my league on Yahoo?

Yes. GREEN18 is not a league platform. It is a live decision board you use alongside the draft you are already in.

What do I need to set up before the draft?

Enter your scoring, roster structure and league format in GREEN18 so the board reflects the league you are actually playing.
