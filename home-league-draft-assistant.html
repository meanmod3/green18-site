How GREEN18 Works

  1. Set Your League.

    Configure the scoring, roster structure and format your league has always used.

  2. Shape Your Draft.

    Set the preferences that matter to you rather than assuming every manager drafts from the same list.

  3. Start Drafting.

    As players are selected, remove them from the board. GREEN18 recalculates the remaining opportunity.

  4. Make the Pick.

    One live view of the players that still matter, instead of rankings, notes and tabs.
