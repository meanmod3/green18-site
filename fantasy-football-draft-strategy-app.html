Strategy That Adapts

Strategy Isn’t a List of Players.

Fantasy football draft strategy is deciding what to do when the board doesn’t unfold the way you expected.

GREEN18 is designed for that exact moment.

Download GREEN18 See How GREEN18 Works

The Plan Changes.

Maybe your target disappears one pick before you.

Maybe value falls into your lap.

Maybe everyone starts drafting quarterbacks.

Maybe nobody does.

Maybe your roster unexpectedly becomes RB-heavy.

A real strategy has to survive contact with the draft room.

GREEN18 helps you adapt without abandoning the logic behind your draft — the point of a live draft assistant.

Make Decisions, Not Predictions.

You don’t need to predict every pick before the draft starts.

You need to recognize the best decision when your turn arrives.

GREEN18 keeps the remaining player pool organized around the draft that’s actually happening.

Which is why ADP is the starting line, not the answer.

What Your Strategy Runs On.

League Settings

Scoring and roster structure define what a good pick even means.

Remaining Pool

The players still on the board are the only ones you can draft.

Your Roster

Every pick you make changes the value of the next one.

How GREEN18 Works

  1. Set Your League.

    Configure the scoring, roster structure and format you actually play.

  2. Shape Your Draft.

    Set the preferences behind your plan before the first pick.

  3. Start Drafting.

    Remove players as they go. The board reorganizes around what’s left.

  4. Adapt the Plan.

    Make the best decision available instead of defending a pre-draft script.

Bring a Strategy That Can Adapt.

A plan is only as good as the board it meets.
