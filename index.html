The Draft Changes Everything.

Before the draft, rankings are predictions.

Once the draft begins, every pick becomes information.

GREEN18 reads that changing context and re-ranks the players still available — a method called Draft-State Valuation.

How GREEN18 Works

  1. Set Your League.

    Configure the scoring, roster structure and league format that materially affect player value.

  2. Shape Your Draft.

    Set the preferences that matter to you, instead of drafting from one universal list.

  3. Start Drafting.

    As players are selected, remove them. Players rise. Players fall. The board keeps moving.

  4. Make the Pick.

    One live view of the players that still matter — instead of six browser tabs.

The Player Didn’t Change. Your Situation Did.

Three quarterbacks go in a row. The fourth didn’t suddenly get better.

He got harder to replace — and a player is worth the amount by which he beats whoever would otherwise fill that slot, so his margin over replacement just rose.

That is the difference between crossing a name off a list and recalculating what’s left.

ADP tells you what people expected. Your draft tells you what they’re doing.

Public rankings describe the average draft. GREEN18 helps you navigate yours.
