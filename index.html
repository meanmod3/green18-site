Green18

Green18 is a fantasy football draft assistant for iPhone. You enter your league's settings, run your draft pick by pick, and it tells you who to take and when — using its own projections and its own projected draft value, built from open football data. It works offline, needs no account, and keeps everything you enter on your device.
